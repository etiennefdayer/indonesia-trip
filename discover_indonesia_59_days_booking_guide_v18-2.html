Remote-work reality

Assuming you keep Swiss office hours 09:00–18:00 CET in November/December. This changes how you should use your days: mornings are your adventure window; local sunsets often overlap your European workday.

🇨🇭 Switzerland
09:00 → 18:00CET · UTC+1
Your reference workday for the trip.
🇮🇩 Java
15:00 → 00:00WIB · UTC+7
Morning hikes, temples and Bromo sunrise fit unusually well before work.
🌴 Bali · Lombok · Flores
16:00 → 01:00WITA · UTC+8
Anything ending after ~16:00 local starts eating into the Swiss workday.

Simple rule: sunrise / breakfast / early-morning excursions = ideal. Full-day or sunset tours = schedule on a weekend or take the afternoon off.

Your 4 boat journeys

The phinisi is now part of the sea-travel plan too. Weather first, vessel second; Penida is still the only small fast-boat crossing you may need.

1

Java → Bali

Ketapang → Gilimanuk · one way

Boat: conventional public Ro-Ro
Sea time: ~45–60 min
Typical foot fare: ~Rp10,600 (≈ €0.52)

This is the crossing I would worry about least. Book through official ASDP/Ferizy and do not plan a tight onward connection.
Open Ferizy ↗How it works ↗
2

Lombok → Bali

Lembar → Padang Bai · one way

Boat: large conventional Ro-Ro
Sea time: ~4–5h
Target: Dharma Ferry VIII/IX if timing and weather fit

Do not wait for a specific ship if another large Ro-Ro has a much better weather window. If the sea is unusually rough, LOP → DPS is the backup.
Ferizy ↗BMKG marine ↗
3

Bali ↔ Nusa Penida

Kusamba ↔ Sampalan · return

Best case: KMP Nusa Jaya Abadi Ro-Ro if back in service
Fallback: Gangga Express
Fast-boat time: ~30 min each way

Keep this flexible. Your personal benchmark says ~2 m in a fast boat is a hard no. Pick a calm morning and move the weekend if necessary.
